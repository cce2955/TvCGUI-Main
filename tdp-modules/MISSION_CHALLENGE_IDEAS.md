# Character challenge mission ideas

These are designed as higher-skill challenges rather than replacements for the
existing basic and BnB missions. Exact routes should be live-tested before the
steps are committed to JSON.

| Character | Mission | Challenge concept |
|---|---|---|
| Ken the Eagle | Flight Control | Use Bird Shoot, Random Flight, an air recatch, and Air Bird Smash without touching the ground between the first projectile and the finisher. |
| Casshan | Friender Relay | Call Friender during a Denko conversion, recatch with 6B or 3C, then finish with Shooting Star Kick into Brutal Axe. |
| Tekkaman | Lancer Recatch | Convert Tek Lancer into Tek Win, relaunch, use two airborne Windmills, and finish with Voltekka Air. |
| Polimar | Emblem Cashout | Build the emblem during the combo, keep the route alive through Razor and Hurricane Destruction Fist, then spend it on Illusion Fist. |
| Yatterman-1 | Shock Delivery | Confirm a charged Yatter Shock, route through Yatter Run and Hop, then finish with Yatter-Wan Flame. |
| Doronjo | Minion Orchestra | Use Clutch, Tree, Rock, and Pummel in one uninterrupted route, with a taunt before the final hit. |
| Ippatsuman | Baseball Cycle | Use Mound, Rainball, Quick Upper, and Grand Slam Attack in one route without repeating the same special strength. |
| Jun the Swan | Yoyo Recall | Start with Bingo or Yoyo, convert through Lightning Kick, use Air Yoyo for the recatch, and finish with Phoenix. |
| Karas | Blade Relay | Complete a route using Tobimizuchi, Ukifune, Yoinagi, Kasha Overhead, and Yato before Hiei Zangetsu. |
| Gold Lightan | Armor Counterattack | Absorb or ignore one incoming hit, immediately punish with Gold Press, then convert into Gold Crash or Finger Crash. |
| Ryu | Fireball Chase | Chase Hado L, connect Donkey Kick before the opponent lands, then finish with Tatsu into Shinkuu. |
| Chun-Li | Charge Conversion | Land SBK H, Baroque cancel the recovery, link Legs C, then finish the air route with Air Legs or Shichei. |
| Batsu | Guts Relay | Use Guts Bullet to start the juggle, perform a two-stage Divekick route, then recatch with Guts Upper before the super ender. |
| Morrigan | Airspace Control | Use Soul Fist, two distinct air-dash directions, Shell Kick, and a super without landing after the launcher. |
| Alex | Grappler's Ladder | Stun with Stungun Near, link Flash Chop, land a grounded Powerbomb, then complete a separate Air Powerbomb conversion. |
| Viewtiful Joe | Bomb Timer | Plant Shocking Pink, activate Slow and Zoom, keep the opponent in hitstun until the bomb detonates, then finish with Air Desperado. |
| Volnutt | Weapon Master | Use forward, backward, and airborne weapon switches while confirming attacks from three different equipped weapons in one combo. |
| Roll | Clean Sweep | Create multiple puddles, sweep them all up for Roll's buff, then complete a buffed combo into Mop Up Turbo. |
| Saki | Ammo Audit | Load and connect three different ammunition or shell states, then cash out with Positron Storm. |
| Soki | Perfect Issen | Counter an attack, confirm Issen, use Oni Tactics to extend, land a second Issen, then finish with Purification. |
| PTX-40A | Full Arsenal | Use Weapon Left Mid, Caliber Execution, L-P Buster, and Tactical Caliber in one corner route. |
| Tekkaman Blade | Intrusion Loop | Route Baselard into Crash Intrude and j.2C, recatch with Blitz, then use Falchion before the final super. |
| Joe the Condor | Lasso Recycle | Use Wild Lasso for the pickup, perform an Air Shuriken and air-dash recatch, then finish with Savage Shot into Condor Magnum. |
| Yatterman-2 | Robot Parade | Set Omochama, convert with Yatter Step and Shield, then finish while This Week's Special Robots is active. |
| Zero | Four Charge Test | Land Hyper Zero Blaster levels 1, 2, 3, and 4 during one mission, with level 4 as the combo finisher. |
| Frank West | Department Store Sweep | Use Zombie Grab, Giant Swing or Grand Slam, Roundhouse, Zombie Charge, and Real Megabuster before Dead Rising. |
| Yami | Three-Phase Study | Boss-only mission: record or punish one unique attack from each Yami phase, then survive the final phase for a fixed duration. |

## Empty or unfinished slots already present

The current mission files have convenient placeholders for new challenges:
Alex has two empty Special Challenge entries, Chun-Li has one, Soki has one,
Tekkaman Blade has two, Roll has an empty Two-Hit Challenge, Batsu has an
unfinished Challenge, and Joe the Condor has a challenge note waiting for a
route.
