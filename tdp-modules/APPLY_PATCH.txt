TvCGUI HUD Information Sets

1. Close TvCGUI.
2. Copy the included tdp-modules folder into TvCGUI-Main.
3. Replace existing files when prompted.
4. Start TvCGUI normally.

HUD information sets:
- CORE: health, meter, recoverable health, meter-generation annotations, damage log, inputs, frames, and moves.
- RESEARCH: CORE plus damage scaling.
- FULL: RESEARCH plus interaction, combo, tag, and Attack Properties.
- CUSTOM: shown automatically after any individual HUD toggle is changed.

The Set button cycles CORE, RESEARCH, and FULL.
RESEARCH is the default set.
Attack Properties defaults to OFF.
Damage scaling rows are branded DMG SCALE.

HUD placement:
- P1 remains flush at the top-left corner.
- P2 remains flush at the top-right corner.
